We took a camera to a Melbourne fight night because written recaps cannot capture the sound of a venue when a perfect lands. The feature follows three players through registration, pools, and a grand finals that went longer than the venue insurance preferred.

## What the tape shows

Local scenes are not "content." They are infrastructure. The stream overlay is homemade. The TO is also the person who brought extra sticks. A visiting player from Adelaide changes the bracket maths and then stays to commentate. That is the Australian FGC in a single sentence.

We are hosting the edit as a video feature rather than a news brief because the point is the room, not the winner's circle. Watch it with the sound on. The commentary is secondary to the chairs scraping when someone takes a round.

## Production notes

Handheld cameras, no cinema lighting, and a promise we would not turn a community hall into a brand activation. If you ran the event: thank you for the wristband. If you want your city next: write to the video desk.
