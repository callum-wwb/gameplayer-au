*Skyrim* is a platform. The video follows three Australian modders — a landscape artist, a combat overhauler, and the person who maintains a wabbajack list so the rest of us do not have to. None of them work for Bethesda. All of them have spent more hours in the Creation Kit than is medically advisable.

## Why film it

Text cannot show a before-and-after of a hold that used to look like porridge. It also cannot show the calm of a good load order booting without a red triangle. We filmed installs, failures, and the moment a city overhaul finally matches the user's memory of 2011.

The feature argues a simple point: unofficial work is part of the game's history now. Anniversary Edition did not end that. It just added another compatibility chart.
