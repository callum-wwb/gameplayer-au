Australians are still searching "ghost of yotei release date" a year after launch. That is not confusion. That is the afterlife of a first-party marketing cycle that taught people to type the question until the storefront answered it.

The answer is boring and useful: **Ghost of Yotei released on Thursday 2 October 2025** in Australia, on **PlayStation 5**, including the Pro. It is a [Sucker Punch](https://www.suckerpunch.com/) game published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. It is **out now**.

## Platforms (the question that will not die)

**PS5 only.** Not Xbox. Not Steam. Not "coming to PC later" as an official date you can set a reminder for. If your house is a Series X and a gaming laptop, you are reading coverage, not a shopping list.

A standalone sequel set in 1600s **Ezo** (Hokkaido), about three hundred years after *Ghost of Tsushima*. You play **Atsu**. Jin Sakai is not the lead. The Yotei Six are the sash.

## Australia: PSN, EB, JB, Plus

Live prices move. The pattern does not:

- **PlayStation Store AU** — digital, tonight, full first-party sticker until a sale actually happens.
- **EB Games** — physical, members, the later preowned copy that is how this country actually finishes first-party games.
- **JB Hi-Fi** — physical, quiet catalogue discounts, good when you are already in the store for a TV.

We do not invent a dollar figure and pretend it is still true next Tuesday. Check the listing. If the deluxe bundle is $30 more for a sash colour, it is a sash colour.

**PlayStation Plus Extra / Premium** is the patience play. Sony eventually puts first-party games on the catalogue. If you bounced off Tsushima's shrines, wait for that window instead of performing urgency in September 2026.

PS5 Pro owners get the Ray Tracing Pro preset. It is a photograph. It is not a reason to upgrade a console.
