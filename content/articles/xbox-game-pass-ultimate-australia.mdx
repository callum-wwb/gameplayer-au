Xbox Game Pass Ultimate Australia is a search for a **price**, not a manifesto. Microsoft's own AU store currently lists **Ultimate at AU$25.95 a month**. That number is from [the official Xbox Game Pass Ultimate page](https://www.xbox.com/en-au/games/store/xbox-game-pass-ultimate/cfq7ttc0khs0) as of this filing. Microsoft has already rewritten the ladder in 2026. If the page says something else on the day you tap subscribe, the page wins.

This is a till guide. It is not a review of a game, and it is not a promise that *GTA 6* will ever sit in the catalogue. For the library we actually cover, start at the [Xbox archive](/xbox/). For an older snapshot of a good indie wave, see [Xbox Game Pass adds a genuinely good indie wave](/xbox-game-pass-adds-indie-wave/).

## The 2026 ladder, in Australian English

Microsoft now sells a stack instead of the old "console / PC / Ultimate" postcard. The live AU comparison we are working from:

| SKU | What we are treating it as | AU monthly, as listed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Game Pass Essential** | Library on console and PC, fewer extras | **AU$12.95** |
| **PC Game Pass** | Windows library, no console | **AU$16.95** (April 2026 restructure reporting; confirm live) |
| **Game Pass Premium** | Console + PC, cloud, slower/smaller day-one promise | **AU$17.95** |
| **Game Pass Ultimate** | The top SKU: library + cloud + partner extras + the day-one first-party pitch | **AU$25.95** |

Those Premium and Essential figures are also on Microsoft's AU storefront next to Ultimate. We are not going to pretend a blog post from April is scripture. Open the [Xbox AU store](https://www.xbox.com/en-AU) before you argue with a housemate.

What Ultimate is *for*: you play on a **Series X/S**, a **Windows PC**, and sometimes a **phone or cloud session** when the NBN is in a mood. You want **EA Play** (and whatever partner stack Microsoft is bundling this quarter) without a second subscription. You actually finish games.

What Ultimate is *not* for: one FIFA session a month and a guilty uninstalled folder.

## When $25.95 is cheaper than a deluxe

Do the ugly arithmetic.

- Ultimate month-to-month is **about $311 a year** if you never cancel.
- A single first-party deluxe on PSN or a premium Nintendo cart in this country still clears **$100+** without blinking.
- If Ultimate gets you **two finished games a month** you would have paid $70+ for, it is a discount disguised as a subscription.
- If it gets you a sports title you already own and a cloud stream you close after twelve minutes, it is a donation to Redmond.

Day-one first-party is the marketing. It is also how we played [Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](/clair-obscur-expedition-33-review/) without performing a midnight retail queue. It is not how you will play [GTA 6](/gta-6-release-date-australia/) in November. Rockstar does not do that.

Call of Duty day-one has already been yanked around in the 2026 restructure conversation. If CoD is the only reason you want Ultimate, read the **current** benefit footnote on Xbox.com, not a 2024 thread.

## Cloud, PC, sharing, the questions Aussies actually type

**Cloud.** Ultimate's pitch includes unlimited cloud gaming. In this country that means: excellent on a good fibre night, comedy on a congested suburban node. Treat cloud as a preview and a travel trick, not as your archive.

**PC.** Ultimate includes the PC library. If you will never touch a console, **PC Game Pass** is the SKU that does not charge you for a box you do not own.

**Can you share it?** Microsoft's household / game-share rules are the thing people mean by "can I share Ultimate with family." They change, and they are not the same as "one login, four suburbs." Use the [Xbox Game Pass FAQ](https://www.xbox.com/en-AU/xbox-game-pass/faq) rather than a Facebook screenshot. We will not invent a share method that gets your account banned.

**Annual codes.** Microsoft's AU default is monthly. Retailers such as **JB Hi-Fi** sell 12-month digital codes for **some** tiers — often Essential, not Ultimate. Read the product title. A cheap year of the wrong SKU is how you spend December in the wrong library.

**EB Games** is the other physical-adjacent ritual: cards, console bundles, the person behind the counter who will ask if you already have an Xbox account. Fine. Still check the SKU.

**Trials.** Microsoft still rotates AU$1 / 14-day trials on lower tiers. Useful as a test. Useless as a lifestyle.

## Ultimate vs PlayStation Plus Extra

We already made this argument in [Best PS5 games 2026](/best-ps5-games-2026/). Plus Extra in Australia is a catalogue you treat like a library. Ultimate is a catalogue **plus** a day-one first-party habit **plus** PC and cloud if you use them.

If the living room is a PS5 and the weekend is [Ghost of Yotei](/ghost-of-yotei-review/) or [Death Stranding 2](/death-stranding-2-review/), Plus is the subscription that matches the furniture. If the living room is a Series X and a decent PC, Ultimate is the one that stops you buying the same RPG twice.

You do not need both unless you are reviewing games for a living. Most households should pick a cabinet.
