"GTA 6 release date Australia" is a search box with a pulse. People type it because Rockstar trained a generation to treat calendars as rumours. As of this filing the date is still the date: **Thursday 19 November 2026**, **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X/S**, worldwide.

That is not a review. Amelia's [Vice City preview](/gta-6-vice-city-preview/) is the hands-on weather report. This is the Australian logistics note. When we have played the shipped build, a score will live on the [GTA 6 hub](/games/gta-6/). Until then, anyone selling you a "GTA 6 review" is writing fiction.

## The date, without the mythology

Rockstar locked **19 November 2026** after the earlier Fall 2025 window moved. Take-Two has kept repeating it in earnings language. We are two months out as of 5 September 2026. Another slip is possible in the way every Rockstar slip is possible. It is not the working assumption.

November 19 is a Thursday. Australian unlocks for big Sony/Microsoft launches are usually that calendar date, often in the evening or the small hours depending on which store you used. We are not inventing a midnight AEDT screenshot. If you need to be in Vice City at the first possible legal second, pre-load and assume you are staying up.

**No PS4. No Xbox One.** If someone at a BBQ tells you otherwise, they are remembering *GTA V*.

**No PC date.** The Windows port will almost certainly exist later. It does not exist as a day-one SKU you can pre-order with a straight face.

## Australia: EB, JB, PSN, Xbox, the code in the box

Live prices move. The pattern since pre-orders opened in June 2026:

- **Standard** listings at Australian storefronts have sat around **$129.95 AUD**.
- **Ultimate / deluxe** bundles cost more for cosmetics, story dressing, and the usual Rockstar garnish. If the extra is not a number you can see on a live product page, do not take it from a screenshot in a Facebook group.
- **Physical is a download code in a box.** There is no disc to lend your cousin. Take-Two has been explicit. You are paying for packaging and a code that has to arrive in time to pre-load.
- **Pre-load:** Take-Two has pointed at **12 November 2026**, a week out. Digital PSN AU and Xbox AU are the clean path. Physical only works if EB, JB, Amazon, or whoever you used actually puts the code in your hand before the 12th.

Where to look, not where we take a cut:

- [PlayStation Store AU](https://store.playstation.com/en-au/)
- [Xbox store](https://www.xbox.com/en-AU)
- **EB Games** and **JB Hi-Fi** for the cardboard ritual

If a listing promises midnight launch *and* in-store pickup *and* a disc, read it twice.
