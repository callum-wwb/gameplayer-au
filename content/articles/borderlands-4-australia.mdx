**Borderlands 4** is the 1,600-search Australian tail of a much bigger worldwide query. Gearbox made it. **2K** published it. It launched **Thursday 11 September 2025** on **PlayStation 5**, **Xbox Series X|S**, and **PC**. The search that remains is the receipt: Standard, Deluxe, Super Deluxe, and whether the DLC ladder is a discount or a hostage note.

This is a till guide. It is not a review of Kairos, and it is not a ranked list of Vault Hunters. GamePlayer has not posted a score. The [Borderlands 4 hub](/games/borderlands-4/) is the collection point until a playthrough exists.

Official box language, not a verdict: a new planet, four launch Vault Hunters, the usual billions-of-guns sentence, co-op. 2K’s buy page is [borderlands.2k.com/borderlands-4/buy](https://borderlands.2k.com/borderlands-4/buy/).

## Platforms

**PS5, Xbox Series, Windows** (Steam and Epic). No PS4. No Xbox One. No Switch SKU as of 13 October 2026.

If a marketplace listing promises a last-gen disc, it is the wrong number in the title or a paperweight.

## The three receipts

Steam AU, checked 13 October 2026 — and the same bands Australian pre-order threads used when 2K announced pricing:

| Edition | Steam AU | What 2K says is inside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | **A$109.95** | The game |
| **Deluxe** | **A$159.95** | Game + **Bounty Pack Bundle** (four post-launch packs: new areas, bosses, cards, cosmetics, vehicles) |
| **Super Deluxe** | **A$199.95** | Deluxe + **Vault Hunter Pack** (two extra hunters, two story packs, more map) + **Ornate Order** cosmetics |

The important sentence is in 2K’s own support article: the Bounty packs and Vault Hunter packs **also sell separately after launch**, and the standalone DLC can cost more than the gap between Standard and Super Deluxe. Australians already did that maths in the announcement week — the combined packs on their own have been quoted near **A$150**. That is how a $110 game becomes a $260 hobby.

[Borderlands 4: What’s Included in my Edition?](https://support.borderlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/44948731427859-Borderlands-4-What-s-Included-in-my-Edition) is the checklist. We are not going to rewrite it as a review.

**Digital-only** on the deluxe SKUs is the other trap. 2K’s edition copy has described Deluxe / Super Deluxe as digital on PS5, Xbox, Steam, or Epic. If you want a box on a shelf, you are probably buying Standard and taking your chances on DLC later.

## When Standard is the adult choice

Buy **Standard** if you want to see whether the loop sticks before you pre-pay four bounty packs.

Buy **Deluxe** only if you already know you finish Borderlands DLC and you have compared the bundle price to the live standalone packs.

Buy **Super Deluxe** only if two extra hunters is the actual reason you are here, not because the product page made $200 feel like a club.

Shift-period Steam sales have already halved these SKUs in other regions. Australia’s October 2026 card is still at RRP on Steam. That can change next week. It is not a reason to panic-buy Super Deluxe today.
