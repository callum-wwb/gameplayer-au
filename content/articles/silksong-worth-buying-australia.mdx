Jess scored [*Hollow Knight: Silksong* a 9.2](/silksong-review/). My job on the news desk is ruder: is it worth *your* money on an Australian storefront this week.

Short answer: **yes, at indie price, if Hallownest already liked you.** No, if you bounced off the first game's runbacks and were hoping Team Cherry had become FromSoftware-lite with a difficulty slider.

## What you are actually paying for

Silksong is not a $79–$129 Nintendo first-party. It is a Team Cherry download. That single fact does more for the value case than any adjective. A 15–20 hour first ending with a nasty optional afterlife is a fair exchange at Steam AU / eShop AU indie money. It is a worse exchange if you only have four hours and a low tolerance for dying in a moss.

Check the live numbers yourself — we do not freeze prices in a CMS:

- **Steam AU** — usually the cheapest digital, plus a sale calendar that actually happens.
- **Nintendo eShop AU** — convenient if the Switch is the only machine you finish games on. Rarely the cheapest.
- **Physical** — if a Limited / special run exists in your suburb, treat it as merch, not a bargain.

## Switch vs PC

I am not restating the review. I am stating the commute.

**Switch** is the correct copy if Silksong will be a handheld habit. Performance in our sessions was stable enough in opening biomes; denser rooms are why you should keep the dock option in your head.

**PC** is the correct copy if you already play Metroidvanias at a desk and you care about the last 10% of frame time. Steam also makes "I'll refund this if the first hour is a brick wall" a real consumer right. eShop does not.

Same game. Same Hornet. Different furniture.

## Who should wait

Wait if you never finished *Hollow Knight*. Play [the original](/games/hollow-knight/) first; it is cheaper, kinder in the opening, and it teaches the language Pharloom assumes you speak.

Wait if you only want a cozy map-cleared. Silksong will not be that in week one.

Do not wait for a "complete edition." Team Cherry does not operate like a live-service calendar. The game that shipped is the game.

## Who should buy tonight

You 100%'d Hallownest. You still think about the tram. You have a Sunday. Buy it.
