**AFL 26** is the search that only really exists in this country — 2,400 Australian lookups a month for a licensed footy game people still file next to EA in their heads. It is not EA. It is **Big Ant Studios** in Melbourne, **Nacon** on the box, and the **AFL** on the licence.

The AFL’s own announcement put it on shelves **Thursday 8 May 2025**. Steam dated the PC build **7 May**. Nintendo’s Switch 2 page put the handheld a year later: **31 March 2026**. All of those dates are in the past. This page is the Australian logistics note, not a brownlow count of the sim.

No review score. The [AFL 26 hub](/games/afl-26/) is the collection point until a GamePlayer playthrough exists.

## What shipped

Official pitch, not a verdict: licensed AFL clubs and players, the 2025-season dressing, local multiplayer, and online that wants a platform subscription on console. PSN’s listing has described **up to four players offline** and online play that requires **PlayStation Plus**. Xbox’s page flags a Game Pass *core* subscription for console multiplayer — that is the online tax, not a copy of the game in the catalogue.

File size on PlayStation’s tracker has sat around **37 GB (PS5)** and **43 GB (PS4)**. Treat those as “leave room,” not scripture.

## Platforms and the Switch 2 lag

**Day one (May 2025):** PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Windows PC.

**Later:** **Nintendo Switch 2** on **31 March 2026**, per [Nintendo AU](https://www.nintendo.com/au/games/nintendo-switch-2/afl-26/).

There was no Switch 1 version in that launch wave. If a marketplace listing claims otherwise, it is either a different year or a different product.

Cross-gen digital on PlayStation is a real SKU — PSN AU sells a **PS4 & PS5** package. Read the product title. A cheap PS4 disc is not automatically a PS5 install.

We do not freeze a Sunday catalogue price and pretend it is still true on Thursday. The RRP pattern has been **ninety-nine dollars**, not the $119 sports-annual default 2K and EA like to open at.

Credits bundles are currency. If you do not play the mode that spends them, they are a donation to Nacon.

## Game Pass, Plus, and the patience myth

As of this filing, AFL 26 is a **purchase** on the PlayStation Store, not a Plus Extra catalogue item. Xbox lists **cloud play** *if you already own the game* and have the right Game Pass SKU — that is streaming a copy you paid for, not a day-one inclusion. Do not cancel a pre-order because a cousin said “it will be on Game Pass.”
