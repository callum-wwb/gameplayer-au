*Baldur's Gate 3* is what happens when a studio treats reactivity as the main feature rather than a bullet point. You can talk, shove, seduce, or explode a problem, and the game usually has a line for it. That is not marketing. That is a production miracle we are still unpacking years later.

## A party worth keeping

The companions are the review. Shadowheart's faith, Astarion's hunger, Karlach's engine, Lae'zel's certainty — they are written as people who will disagree with you and still camp next to you. Origin runs make the second playthrough a different novel.

Combat is 5e with Larian grease on top. Surfaces, elevation, and the joy of shoving a cultist off a cliff. Honour Mode turns that toybox into a thriller. Even Explorer difficulty respects the systems instead of deleting them.

## The cost of density

Act 3 can buckle under its own quest log. Split-screen on console is a generous idea that occasionally fights the UI. And you will save-scum. Everyone save-scums. The game is too proud of its dice for you not to.

## Verdict

**9.8/10**. The RPG benchmark until someone is brave enough to spend this much on conversation trees. If you have been waiting for a sign to start a new campaign, this is it — again.
