Amelia's [Mario Kart World review](/mario-kart-world-review/) is the play argument (8.8). This is the till argument.

## The hardware gate

World is a **Switch 2** game. If your living room is still OLED or Lite, you are shopping for *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe*, not a sequel that will not boot. That sounds obvious. Australian product pages still get this wrong in the comments.

If you do not own the Switch 2, read our [launch lineup](/nintendo-switch-2-launch-lineup-australia/) before you treat a Kart as a console reason. A household that will also play [Donkey Kong Bananza](/donkey-kong-bananza-review/) has a better maths story than a household that wants twelve online races and then a dusty dock.

## Where to buy it in Australia

Live prices move. The pattern does not:

- **Nintendo eShop AU** — instant, full sticker, sales are shy.
- **EB Games** — physical, preowned later, the occasional member price.
- **JB Hi-Fi** — physical, quiet catalogue discounts, good when you are already in the store for a TV.
- **Amazon AU / Big W** — chase only when the landed price actually undercuts EB/JB. Watch shipping on "bargain" listings.

A bundle (console + World) is worth it when the Kart is effectively cheap against standalone. It is not worth it when the bundle is just Nintendo hiding the same two stickers in one box.

## Stock, used copies, and the week-two reality

Launch weekends in Australia still produce the empty-shelf photo. Week three produces the used copy. If you can wait, you will pay less and you will still have the same rainbow road.

Online play needs a Nintendo Switch Online membership. That is a second subscription on top of whatever you already pay for Plus or Game Pass. Local four-player does not. If your NBN node hates Saturday nights, World is still a couch game first — which is the review's actual point.

## Does it replace 8 Deluxe?

On Switch 2: yes, it becomes the default cartridge you put in when people come over.

On Switch 1: no replacement exists. Keep 8 Deluxe. Invite people who have not upgraded. Do not let a search result for "mario kart world review" talk you into a console you will not feed.
