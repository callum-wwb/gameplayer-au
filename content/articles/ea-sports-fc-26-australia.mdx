**EA Sports FC 26** is what Australians still type as “FIFA” when they want a price. Semrush’s AU database gives **FC 26** 9,900 monthly lookups and the long-tail **EA Sports FC 26** another 2,900. Same annual. Same problem: too many SKUs, and a Standard price that depends on whether you are looking at PSN or Steam.

EA Canada and EA Romania developed it. **Electronic Arts** published it. Standard landed **26 September 2025**. It is out. This page is the Australian receipt.

No review score. Ultimate Team is not a verdict. The [FC 26 hub](/games/ea-sports-fc-26/) is the collection point.

## Dates

- **Standard:** 26 September 2025 worldwide. Xbox AU and PlayStation AU listed **25 September** depending on how the storefront stores the timezone.
- **Ultimate Edition:** early access the week prior — the usual EA “pay to start on Friday instead of Thursday” window.

If you wanted launch-week Ultimate Team, that window is closed. If you wanted the match engine on a sale, October 2026 is the more interesting month.

## Platforms

**PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4** (PS5 Pro Enhanced on Sony’s page). **Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One** as a dual-entitlement pair on Xbox’s Standard SKU. **Windows PC.** Nintendo has sold FC on Switch in this generation — read the listing; the portable build is not the same conversation as the PS5 one.

EA’s dual-entitlement footnote lives on [ea.com](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/dual-entitlement). Cross-gen on Xbox is the version that does not make you buy the annual twice when you upgrade the box. Cross-gen on PlayStation is a product title you have to read.

## The edition ladder

EA’s naming is a slot machine. In Australia you will see some mix of these:

| SKU | What it is | AU ballpark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | The game | **A$109.95** on PlayStation AU; **A$39.00** on Steam AU as of 13 October 2026; Xbox has been on rotating sale tags |
| **Ultimate Edition** | Standard plus the early-access week and a load of Ultimate Team / Career starter currency | Launch prestige money — confirm the live Xbox/PSN card |
| **The World’s Game Edition** | Prestige on top of prestige: Season Points, Gold Starting XI, ICON/Hero/TOTS-style picks | PlayStation AU has listed **A$239.95** |

World’s Game is a pack of **untradeable** Ultimate Team items. It does not make Career Mode a different sport. If you do not play Football Ultimate Team, it is a very expensive gold pack.

Steam’s **A$39** Standard (13 October 2026) is the number that should stop anyone paying PSN’s $109.95 *if they were going to play on PC anyway*. Console lock-in is the only reason the full sticker still exists.

## EA Play, Game Pass, FC Points

**EA Play** (bundled on some Game Pass SKUs) is the trial door, historically about ten hours. Useful as a test. Useless as a plan to “just finish Career.” Confirm the live trial length on EA’s site; they change it.

Game Pass Ultimate is not a promise that the full annual sits in the catalogue all year. Read [our Ultimate guide](/xbox-game-pass-ultimate-australia/), then the Xbox comparison chart, then decide.

**FC Points** are the other till. Steam AU lists them from **A$1.85 (100)** to **A$79.95 (5,900)**. That ladder is how a $39 game becomes a $150 winter. We will not moralise it. We will not pretend it is part of Standard.

Online play on PlayStation wants **Plus**. EA wants an **EA account**. Both are footnotes people skip and then email the desk about.
