The search is **“Battlefield 6 vs Black Ops 7”** — only 1,600 Australian lookups a month, but it is the honest one. Two $110-class shooters, two publishers, two definitions of “the new one.” Australians type it because the living room only has one Friday night.

This is a comparison of **platforms, editions, and subscriptions**. It is not a review of gunfeel, and it is not a ranked list. GamePlayer has not posted a score for [Battlefield 6](/games/battlefield-6/) or [Black Ops 7](/games/black-ops-7/). Anyone selling you a winner with a decimal is writing ahead of this desk.

If your house still has a **PS4** as the main box, the comparison is over: only Black Ops 7 sold you a SKU. If the house is current-gen and already pays for **Game Pass Ultimate**, start on the Xbox AU product pages, not on a deluxe pre-order.

## Platforms and the Australian unlock

**Battlefield 6** launched on a single worldwide clock: 10 October 2025, 8am PT. In the eastern states that was **Saturday 11 October, 2:00am AEDT**. EA documented it; PC Gamer’s launch-time table matches. There is no PS4, no Xbox One, and no “cross-gen disc” to argue about.

**Black Ops 7** staggered. Xbox AU says **14 November 2025**. PlayStation AU said **13 November**. Steam said **21 November**. That is three calendars for one annual, which is why people still type the name.

PC both ways: **Secure Boot / TPM** footnotes exist on both publishers’ support pages. An older tower can fail the door before it fails the frame rate.

## Money, without the trailer

Both Standards sit on **A$109.95** on Steam AU today. That is not a coincidence. It is the 2025–26 Australian AAA default.

The upsells are cosmetics-and-pass bundles with different names:

- **BF6 Phantom** — Battlefield Pro token, Phantom squad skins, weapon packages, XP boosts. EA’s Steam card is the list.
- **BO7 Vault** — one season of BlackCell, Guild Override camo, operators, Mastercraft weapons, Ultra GobbleGums, Permanent Unlock Token. [Activision’s editions article](https://support.activision.com/black-ops-7/articles/black-ops-7-editions) is the list.

Neither deluxe is a different multiplayer. If you do not care about the pass on week one, Standard (or a subscription) is the SKU.

Physical day-one for Battlefield was the usual Australian sport: Target / JB / Amazon undercut EB’s $109.95. Those windows close. Check the week you care.

## Game Pass, EA Play, Plus

**Black Ops 7** is the one Xbox has been willing to put on the Game Pass poster. If Ultimate is already on the credit card, that is the cheapest *legal* try. Catalogue inclusion is not a forever contract — read the live footnote, then our [Ultimate Australia guide](/xbox-game-pass-ultimate-australia/).

**Battlefield 6** is EA. EA Play Pro and Game Pass partnerships have covered Battlefield before. They have also pulled shooters. We will not screenshot a 2025 launch blog and call it October 2026 policy. Open the live catalogue.

PlayStation Plus is online-play rent on both, not a copy of either.

## How to choose without a score

Choose **Battlefield 6** if the group already talks in squads, vehicles, and large maps, and every box in the house is current-gen.

Choose **Black Ops 7** if someone is still on last-gen, if Game Pass is the budget, or if the year you want is prestige / Zombies / a numbered CoD season.

Choose **neither deluxe** if the extra forty dollars is a skin sheet.

You do not need both unless you review games or you have two friend groups that refuse to overlap. Most households should pick a loop.
