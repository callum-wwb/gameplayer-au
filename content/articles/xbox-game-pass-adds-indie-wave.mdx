Game Pass has a reputation problem: too many day-one monsters, not enough games you finish. This month's wave is the correction. Microsoft added a cluster of indie titles — a tactics roguelike, a narrative walking sim from a Melbourne studio, and a co-op puzzler that should have been a Plus Extra exclusive if Sony were faster.

## Why this drop matters

Subscriptions win when they reduce regret. A 90-minute game with a point of view is a better advert for the service than another 80-hour map. The Melbourne title in particular is the kind of local credit we like to see on a global storefront: shipped, paid, and not buried under a sports menu.

PC Game Pass and console share the list this time, which avoids the usual "which SKU is real" confusion. Cloud streaming remains optional, not mandatory, and that is the correct hierarchy.

## What we are playing first

The tactics roguelike. It has the density of a PC niche hit and the onboarding of something that expects a controller. If you only install one game from the slate, make it the one that will not ask you for a battle pass in week two.

We will review the local studio's title separately. For now, the news is simple: Game Pass remembered that small can be the feature.
