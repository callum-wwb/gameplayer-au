*Balatro* looks like a pub game and behaves like a spreadsheet that learned charisma. LocalThunk took poker hands, jokers that rewrite maths, and a run structure so tight it feels handmade even on the twentieth loss. We have not stopped dealing.

## Jokers as design documents

Every joker is a thesis about what a hand can mean. Stack two or three and the scoring language changes. The joy is not gambling. The joy is engineering a machine that prints chips and then watching it eat a boss blind.

On Switch and mobile it is lethal. Five-minute antes become an hour. PlayStation and Xbox versions keep the neon and lose none of the snap. PC is where the community theory-crafting lives.

## The warning label

There is no story to pull you out. There is only the next ante. If you have a compulsive streak, set a timer that is not a joker. The game is fair. Your sleep schedule is not.

## Verdict

**9.1/10**. A modern classic that happens to be about poker the way *Into the Breach* is about chess: the rules are a launching pad. Deal in.
