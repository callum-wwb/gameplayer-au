**Kirby Air Riders** is the 4,400-search-a-month Nintendo query that still behaves like a pre-order page. It is not. The game released **Thursday 20 November 2025** on **Nintendo Switch 2** only. If your cabinet is a PS5, a Series X, or a day-one Switch in a draw, this is coverage, not a shopping list.

Masahiro Sakurai directed it. **Bandai Namco Studios** and **Sora Ltd.** built it. **Nintendo** publishes it. The copyright line on the Australian store is the honest one: © Nintendo / SORA © Nintendo / HAL Laboratory, Inc. The cover on this page is an **original editorial card** — pastel islands, empty air-lanes, no characters — because we do not have a licensed Kirby still and we will not scrape one.

No review score. The [Kirby Air Riders hub](/games/kirby-air-riders/) holds the guide until a playthrough exists.

## The platform sentence people keep retyping

**Switch 2 exclusive.** Not Switch 1. Not PlayStation. Not Xbox. Not Steam. Nintendo’s product pages are boring on purpose: [nintendo.com/au](https://www.nintendo.com/au/games/nintendo-switch-2/kirby-air-riders/) and [store.nintendo.com.au](https://store.nintendo.com.au/au/kirby-air-riders.html).

If a marketplace seller offers a “Switch OLED compatible” cart, they are selling you a different decade or a different game.

## What the box is selling (not a verdict)

Nintendo’s own copy, compressed:

- **Air Ride** — a race. Local and online fields; you pick a rider and a machine and the machine wants top speed unless you tell it otherwise.
- **City Trial** — the GameCube memory. Up to **16 online** or **eight local wireless** in Skyah, five minutes of pickups and field events, then a Stadium.
- **Boost Charge** — brake and turn, then dump the gauge for a dash. That is the handling sentence.

Riders and machines are the roster Nintendo put on the back of the box. We are not going to recap them as if we had time-trialled the Warp Star.

Classification in Australia has sat at **G / PG** depending on the retailer feed (“mild fantasy themes, violence and online interactivity”). Check the classification marking on the listing you actually buy.
