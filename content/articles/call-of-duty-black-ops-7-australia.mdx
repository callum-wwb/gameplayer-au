Australians still search **“Call of Duty Black Ops 7”** eleven months after launch because the annual taught a generation to type the name until a storefront answered. The calendar part is finished. **Black Ops 7 is out.** The useful question in October 2026 is which door you use and how much Activision wants for the extras.

This is a till guide from the news desk. It is not a review, not a loadout bible, and not a ranked list of maps. When GamePlayer has played the shipped build, a score will live on the [Black Ops 7 hub](/games/black-ops-7/). Until then, treat any “BO7 review” with a number on it as someone else’s homework — or fiction.

## The dates, without the trailer voice

Publisher pages do not all agree on the hour, which is why the search box never cooled down.

- **Xbox AU** lists **14 November 2025** for Xbox Series, Xbox One, and Windows.
- **PlayStation AU** listed **13 November 2025** for PS5 and PS4.
- **Steam AU** dates the PC package **21 November 2025**.

If you bought on console last November, you already know which night your download finished. If you are on Steam and thought you missed a week, that gap is real. There is no PS3, no 360, and no Switch SKU to argue about.

Official product copy: Treyarch and Raven Software, 2035, **David Mason**, the city of **Avalon**, a co-op campaign, **16 six-versus-six maps** and two 20v20 maps at launch, Omnimovement, and Round-Based Zombies in the Dark Aether. That is the box. It is not a verdict.

## Platforms (including the last-gen ones)

**PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4. Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. PC** via Steam, Battle.net, and Xbox on PC.

Digital **Cross-Gen** editions on Xbox and PlayStation are the version that plays on both boxes in the same family. Physical is messier, and Activision has been explicit:

- **Xbox physical** is a cross-gen bundle (Series and One).
- **PS5 physical** is PS5 only.
- **PS4 physical** is a cross-gen bundle that includes a PS5 digital code — and the disc still matters if you want to run the PS5 install.

If a Facebook listing promises a single plastic disc that “does every PlayStation,” walk away.

PC has the usual 2025 tax: **TPM 2.0** and **Secure Boot** are in Activision’s footnotes. Read [Activision’s support note](https://support.activision.com/tpm) before you assume an older tower will launch.

## Standard versus Vault, in Australian dollars

Live prices move. The pattern that has held:

| Edition | What you are actually buying | AU ballpark, as listed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cross-Gen / Standard** | The game | **A$109.95** on Steam AU as of 13 October 2026; PSN AU has used the same RRP |
| **Vault Edition** | Standard plus one season of BlackCell, camo, operators, Mastercraft weapons, Ultra GobbleGums, Permanent Unlock Token | Xbox AU has listed **from A$149.95** |

Vault does not unlock a secret campaign. It front-loads a battle-pass season and cosmetics. If you already subscribe to Game Pass and only wanted to *play*, Vault is an upsell, not an entry ticket.

## Game Pass, Plus, and the phone-number footnote

Xbox’s Australian page still headlines **Game Pass**. That is the cheapest way to *try* the thing if your household already pays for Ultimate or the PC SKU. It is not a promise that CoD stays in the catalogue forever. Read [our Ultimate Australia guide](/xbox-game-pass-ultimate-australia/) for the ladder, then confirm the live benefit footnote.

PlayStation Plus is the online-play tax on console, not a copy of the game. Activision also wants an **Activision account** and, in their legal block, a **mobile number** tied to that account. That is a support article, not a rumour.

Storage: plan for a large install plus mandatory updates. We are not going to invent a gigabyte figure that a Tuesday patch will make wrong.

## If you are choosing a shooter instead

Black Ops 7 is the 6v6 / Zombies / prestige loop, and it still talks to last-gen hardware. [Battlefield 6](/battlefield-6-vs-black-ops-7-australia/) is the 64-player, vehicles, current-gen-only argument. They are not interchangeable, even if the search box treats them as one mood.
