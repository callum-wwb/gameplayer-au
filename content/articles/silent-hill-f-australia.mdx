**Silent Hill f** is the optional eighth piece in this go-live batch because Australians actually search it — 14,800 monthly lookups, more than half the titles we were asked to cover. NeoBards Entertainment developed it. **Konami** published it. Steam dated the PC build **24 September 2025**; most worldwide calendars called it **25 September**. It is out.

The marketing sentence is Hinako, a hometown in fog, 1960s Japan, puzzles, monsters. That is Konami’s poster. This is the storefront.

No review score. Horror is the genre where borrowed adjectives do the most damage. GamePlayer will not paraphrase someone else’s nightmare and put a number on it. The [Silent Hill f hub](/games/silent-hill-f/) holds the guide until a playthrough exists.

## Platforms

**PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Windows PC.** No PS4. No Xbox One. No Switch SKU as of this filing.

If the living room is last-gen, you are reading a news note, not a checkout page.

The fifteen-dollar Deluxe gap is the one to read twice. If Deluxe is artbook / soundtrack / cosmetic dressing — Konami’s live product card is the list — it is not a second campaign. The dual packs only make sense if you do not already own the 2024 *Silent Hill 2* remake.

Console RRP in this country usually opens at the $99–$109 band for a Konami current-gen exclusive-feeling release. We are not going to invent a PSN sticker that a weekend sale will contradict. Check:
